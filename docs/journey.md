# The Wingle Journey — Making Travel Better

Wingle was founded with a clear mission: **make travel better**. Not just the flight itself, but the full experience — before, during, and after. That mission has stayed constant even as the products, audiences, and business model have evolved.

This is the story of how we got here.

---

## Timeline

### 2023 — The Mission Takes Shape

Two co-founders — Pol and Iñigo — set out to improve how people experience air travel. The first product they chose to build was an **offline, peer-to-peer chat app for airplane passengers** — a way to turn dead flight time into genuine connection using mesh networking, no Wi-Fi required.

But the chat was always a starting point, not the destination. The bigger ambition was to build a company that could make every part of flying — from airport arrival to landing at your destination — more comfortable, more social, and more human.

The year was spent building core technology, defining the brand, and laying the groundwork for a commercial launch.

---

### Early 2024 — Preparing for Takeoff

With the app taking shape, Wingle secured its first airline advertising deal — seatback ads on European flights — and assembled a creative team for the launch campaign. The iOS app went live, Android development accelerated, and the company applied for ENISA certification as an emerging company.

Even at this early stage, the team was building B2C-first while keeping the door open to how the same ecosystem could eventually serve travel professionals. As the Cockpit Diaries noted at the time: *"Wingle is born with a B2C aspiration, but we cannot close the door to a B2B offering."*

**June 2024** — Wingle attended **South Summit** in Madrid, opening early conversations with airlines, investors, and ecosystem partners.

---

### July 2024 — Launch

**July 1, 2024** — Wingle launched commercially across Europe.

Seatback ads went live on six Vueling aircraft, reaching over 250,000 passengers that summer. The Android app followed shortly after. To celebrate, Wingle hid boarding passes across airports as part of a social media activation campaign.

The focus was entirely on consumers: download the app, chat with fellow passengers, discover a more social flight experience.

---

### Summer 2024 — Traction and Partnerships

The first months after launch were about proving demand and building momentum.

- **BMG Music partnership** — Curated destination playlists appeared in the app, giving users a soundtrack for their trip.
- **"Meet at the Airport"** — A new feature let users connect with other Winglers before boarding, extending the social experience beyond the cabin.
- **Media coverage** — Features in El Mundo, El Confidencial, Condé Nast Traveler, La Vanguardia, and international outlets brought organic downloads and brand visibility.
- **6,500+ active users** by mid-August, with a record of 21 people using Wingle simultaneously on a single London–Malaga flight.

**September 2024** — Wingle expanded airline presence to **Iberia Express** (200,000+ additional passenger impressions) and launched the **Wingle Pilots** ambassador program, rewarding users for spreading the word.

---

### Autumn 2024 — Expanding Beyond the Chat

With initial traction validated, Wingle began delivering on its broader mission — making travel better at every stage of the journey.

- **Lounge access** — VIP airport lounges became available to all Wingle users.
- **Baggage storage** — Users could book luggage storage in destination cities.
- **Contextual intelligence** — The app started recognizing where in the travel journey a user was and surfacing the right services for that moment.
- **World Aviation Festival (Amsterdam)** — Wingle attended as an invited startup, opening conversations with airlines and unlocking new partnerships.
- **First hire** — Miguel Icaza joined from Miami Ad School to lead marketing and activations.

By November 2024, the user base had grown to approximately **15,000 active users** — all without seatback ads for that period. The team announced plans for a fundraising round.

---

### December 2024 — A Year in Review

Wingle closed 2024 with **nearly 20,000 active users**, just five months after launch. A partnership with **Terravision** put Wingle ads on ~40 airport shuttle buses across Rome and Milan.

The year had proven traction for the chat and early travel services. But it also surfaced a challenge: users loved the chat, but engagement was short-lived and conversion to paid services was low. The underlying mission — make travel better — was clear. The question was how to deliver on it in a way that sustained the business.

---

### Early 2025 — Wingle Pass and the Full Travel Companion

**February 2025** — Wingle launched **Wingle Pass**, a credits-based system giving users on-demand access to premium airport services: lounge access, fast-track security, baggage storage, and more. No subscription — buy credits and spend them when it makes sense for your trip.

This was a major step in making the app a full travel companion, not just a flight-time social tool. The chat remained, but it was now part of a broader offering covering the entire journey.

The same month, the team traveled to the **Aviation Festival Asia in Singapore**, pitching to Asian airlines and expanding international conversations. In the Cockpit Diaries, the founders were candid about the economics: *"Pushing a B2C business is a high-spend venture that requires deep pockets. We've been looking at how we can do more with the powerful tech platform and partner network that we've built."*

**March 2025** — **Land & Laundry** launched: a service that picks up, washes, and returns your travel clothes within 48 hours of landing. Available initially in Madrid, Barcelona, and Valencia.

---

### Spring 2025 — The Retrofit

In Cockpit Diaries Episode 11, the team described the turning point plainly:

> *"People liked the Wingle app; that's a fact. But the chat is not enough. People were using Wingle for the flight itself, but they were not using any of the additional services and therefore not converting into paying users."*

The goal became clear: reshape Wingle into a full companion for air travel, covering the whole journey:

- **Before your flight**: Fast-track security, VIP lounge access
- **During your flight**: Offline chat (the original feature)
- **After your flight**: Luggage storage, Land & Laundry, and support for flight delay compensation workflows

---

### Early 2025 — The B2B Opportunity Emerges

By late February 2025, while the consumer app evolved, the team recognized that the infrastructure they had created for consumers — supplier networks, curation logic, service coordination — had enormous value for a different audience: **travel agencies**.

Luxury travel agencies were already coordinating premium airport services for their clients — lounges, meet & assist, VVIP terminal experiences — but doing so through fragmented suppliers, manual processes, and inconsistent quality. Wingle could solve that.

This insight marked the beginning of **Wingle Pro**: a B2B service that gives travel agencies a single partner for premium airport services globally. Rather than building something new from scratch, the team repurposed the platform, supplier relationships, and quality intelligence they had already developed.

The key advantages for agencies were clear from the start:
- One partner for everything premium at the airport, anywhere in the world.
- In-house quality intelligence that filters and recommends only up-to-standard services.
- Flexible integration — Wingle adapts to each agency's existing workflow rather than imposing a rigid technology stack.

The goal was clear: A one-stop partner for premium airport services worldwide, designed for travel agencies — especially luxury and high-end operators. Wingle Pro offers three service tiers:

- **Lounges + Fast Track** — Curated access to 700+ lounges globally, quality-filtered by Wingle's in-house intelligence so agencies can trust every option meets a high standard.
- **Meet & Assist** — A personal assistant meets travelers at the aircraft door, expedites security and passport control, and escorts them to the VIP lounge. Available for departures, arrivals, and connections.
- **VVIP** — Private terminal experience. Private check-in, private security, and a car to the aircraft stairs where available, away from the main terminal flow.

Agencies send a request with route, timing, and traveler details. Wingle confirms curated options and coordinates delivery. No rigid technology required — the service adapts to how each agency already works.

---

### Late 2025 – Early 2026 — Wingle Atlas

Between December 2025 and January 2026, a second B2B product emerged: **Wingle Atlas**.

Atlas was born from a different observation. Travel agencies — especially those designing bespoke trips — were still sending travelers static PDF itineraries: walls of text with generic hotel descriptions and disconnected logistics. The experience didn't match the quality of the trip being sold.

Atlas transforms those static documents into **dynamic, visual, human-first travel itineraries** that travelers actually enjoy using. Travel designers can attach tips, voice notes, photos, and live information to each moment of a trip. And every itinerary feeds a shared knowledge base, so destination expertise compounds across the agency over time.

Together with Wingle Pro, Atlas forms the **Wingle for Travel Professionals** offering — a suite that addresses both the airport experience and the trip narrative, the two areas where agencies can most directly elevate their clients' travel.

---

### Today — One Mission, Two Paths

Wingle is now a company with two distinct audiences and a shared mission: **make travel better**.

**For travelers**: the Wingle app is the tool to improve your flight experience — from premium airport services to in-flight connection — all through a simple, credits-based model with no subscriptions.

**For travel professionals**: Wingle Pro provides the premium airport service infrastructure agencies need, with flexible integration that adapts to each agency's workflow. Wingle Atlas gives them a modern itinerary tool and shared knowledge system. Together, they enable agencies to deliver exceptional travel without building the operations themselves.

This website — `letswingle.com` — is the home for all of it. A single destination where every audience finds what they need and understands what Wingle is, what it offers, and how to take the next step.

---

## What Has Never Changed

Through every stage — from a chat app to a travel platform, from B2C to B2B — the core has stayed the same:

- **The mission**: making travel better, at every stage of the journey.
- **The approach**: curated quality over exhaustive options. We don't list everything; we recommend what meets the bar.
- **The energy**: two co-founders, a small team, moving fast with conviction and transparency. The Cockpit Diaries have documented every win, stumble, and lesson publicly since day one.

The journey continues. We're still in the cockpit, and there's a lot of airspace ahead.
