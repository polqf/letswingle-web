As we write this, we're currently flying just over Athens, we're at 34.000 feet and it's -59ºC outside 🥶. We're flying back from the Aviation Festival Asia in Singapore, a great chance to get the dicussion going with Asian airlines. We pitched, we met fellow startups, opened leads with airlines, got interviewed by media and also... had a blast.

February has been the month when we've begun to look at things with a broader view, adapting to the context and reality. And that reality is that, while traction to date has worked well, pushing a B2C business is a high-spend venture that requires deep pockets. So, while we balance resources and reach, we've been looking at how we can do more with the powerful tech platfrom and partner network that we've built.

Our goal in Wingle is to make the flying experience more comfortable, enjoyable and fun than it is today.

The in-flight chat is a huge part of it, but not only. 

🎇 Welcome WinglePass. 🎇 

A flexible, on demand service that gives you access to lounges, fast track, baggage storage... everything related to air travel. For those trips when you want to upgrade your travel, at discounted proces. Purchase credits and use them to book anything for your next trip. All in one place.

We'll be launching the latest app release with WinglePass in the next couple of days. We're confident that there's great value for our users and that at the same time, will be a good way to increase app monetization.

Hope you can try it out!!
