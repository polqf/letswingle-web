Welcome our *"land & laundry"* service. 

Land, get home and get all your dirty clothes from your trip picked up, washed and back to you in less than 48 hours!

We know one of the biggest hassles after arriving from a long (or not so long) trip is having to do all the laundry, and we're taking the hassle away!

Simply select the collection date and time, select whether you'll be sending clothes from a small cabin bag or a checked in suitcase and *get them picked up* at home. In *less than 48hrs later*, they're back at your doorstep, clean and folded.

As always, you can use your Wingle Pass credits to book this service. For now, it's available in *Madrid, Barcelona and Valencia*, and we'll be soon opening new locations.

*Top up your Wingle Pass credits on the app and start flying better!*

____________________________


### Terms and conditions

Allowed clothing:
- Must be washable at 30ºC in a standard washing machine.
- Must be dryer-safe without the risk of shrinking.

The following items are not allowed:
- Clothing with embellishments like sequins or similar decorations.
- Formal wear requiring dry cleaning, such as evening dresses, suits, or ties.
- Sweaters made from mixed fabrics, as they may shrink in the dryer.

* Currently only available in Madrid, Barcelona and Valencia.
